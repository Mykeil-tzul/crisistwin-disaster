How to read this map

A satellite can flag fire, agricultural burning, or another heat source. Multiple detections can come from the same place. A blank map can reflect cloud cover, the timing of a satellite pass, or the filters you chose.

Data and coverage

California’s 58 counties use Census boundaries. County populations give geographic context. They don’t count people affected by a fire. Satellite detections come from NOAA‑20 VIIRS and are checked hourly.
